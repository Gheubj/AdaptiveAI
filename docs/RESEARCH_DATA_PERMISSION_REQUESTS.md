Subject: Clarification of xRouteBench permissions for open routing research

like to evaluate prompt-aware routing with xRouteBench. Could you please confirm:

1. the dataset license and exact version/commit covered;
2. whether research use is permitted;
3. whether commercial use is permitted;
4. whether redistribution of original rows is permitted;
5. whether redistribution of transformed/derived rows is permitted;
6. required attribution and citation text;
7. whether model outputs, prompts, or benchmark labels carry separate upstream restrictions;
8. whether trained weights, statistics, and aggregate benchmark results may be published; and
9. whether any privacy, removal, access-control, or downstream deletion obligations apply.

We will not redistribute or train on the data until these points are documented. Thank you.
